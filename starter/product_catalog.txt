Customer Support Product Catalog and Policy Notes

Loyalty tiers:
Bronze members receive a 2 percent tier discount.
Silver members receive a 5 percent tier discount.
Gold members receive a 10 percent tier discount and priority email support.
Platinum members receive a 15 percent tier discount, free expedited shipping, early product access, and premium return handling.

Points policy:
Customers may redeem loyalty points in 500 point blocks.
Every 100 points is worth 1 USD.
Points redemption is capped at 50 percent of the original order total.
Tier discounts and points redemption may be combined.

Return and refund policy:
Delivered orders are eligible for automated refunds inside a 30 day return window when the item is unused or defective.
Orders that are still processing or in transit should be handled by cancellation or shipment-intercept workflows instead of refunds.
Refunds usually post to the original payment method within 5 business days.
